The maze wants you lost. The thing inside it wants you found.

The green doorway is your way out. Your explored path stays faintly visible.

03

The Shadow wakes after a delay, then gets faster. Dead ends are death.

Your heartbeat quickens when it is close. Sound is your first warning.
